Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about bike insurance in Pakistan.

Bike insurance typically covers theft of the motorcycle, accidental damage (collision, overturning), fire, natural disasters, and third-party liability. The exact coverage depends on the plan you choose — our Basic plan covers third-party liability, while our Premium plan covers everything including roadside assistance.

We insure all types of motorcycles available in Pakistan — from Honda CD 70 and CG 125 to Yamaha YBR, Suzuki GS 150, and imported heavy bikes. Whether your bike is 70cc or 250cc+, we have coverage plans for you.

Filing a claim is simple: 1) Contact us via WhatsApp or call our 24/7 helpline, 2) Submit required documents (FIR for theft, or accident photos/report), 3) Our claims team reviews and processes your claim within 3-7 working days. We guide you through every step.

Bike insurance premiums start from as low as Rs 999/year for basic third-party coverage. Comprehensive coverage (theft + accidental damage) typically ranges from Rs 2,499 to Rs 4,999 per year, depending on your bike's make, model, and market value. Use our quote tool above to get an instant estimate!

While comprehensive bike insurance is not strictly enforced, third-party liability insurance is required by law under the Motor Vehicles Act. However, given the high theft rates in Pakistan's major cities, we strongly recommend comprehensive coverage for financial protection.

Yes! We insure both new and used/second-hand bikes. For used bikes, coverage is based on the current market value. Bikes up to 10 years old are eligible for comprehensive coverage, and older bikes can get third-party liability protection.
